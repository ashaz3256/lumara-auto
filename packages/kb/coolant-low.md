# Low Coolant Warning

## What is Low Coolant Warning?
The low coolant warning indicates the cooling system is not maintaining proper coolant level, which can lead to overheating.

## Common Causes
1. **Coolant leak** - Most common cause
2. **Evaporation** - Normal over time, especially in hot climates
3. **Head gasket failure** - Internal coolant leak
4. **Radiator leak** - External leak from radiator
5. **Hose failure** - Cracked or loose coolant hoses
6. **Water pump leak** - Seal failure in water pump

## Symptoms
- Low coolant warning light
- Coolant level below minimum mark
- Engine temperature rising
- Sweet smell from engine bay
- Visible coolant leaks under car
- Overheating if severe

## Safe Checks
1. **Coolant level** - Check reservoir and radiator (when cool)
2. **Visual inspection** - Look for leaks under car
3. **Hose condition** - Check for cracks or soft spots
4. **Radiator cap** - Ensure tight and not damaged
5. **Oil condition** - Check for milky oil (head gasket leak)

## Severity Assessment
- **LIMIT** - Safe to drive short distances if level adequate
- **STOP** - If level very low or dropping rapidly
- Monitor temperature gauge closely
- Add coolant if available

## Professional Diagnosis Required
- Cooling system pressure test
- Leak detection and repair
- Head gasket inspection if internal leak
- Radiator inspection and repair
- Hose replacement if needed
