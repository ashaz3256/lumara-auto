# Check Engine Light (Generic)

## What is Check Engine Light?
The Check Engine Light (CEL) or Malfunction Indicator Lamp (MIL) indicates the engine management system has detected a problem that could affect emissions or performance.

## Common Causes
1. **Oxygen sensor failure** - Most common cause, affects fuel mixture
2. **Catalytic converter issues** - Reduced efficiency or failure
3. **Mass airflow sensor** - Incorrect air/fuel ratio readings
4. **Spark plug/ignition coil problems** - Misfires or poor combustion
5. **Fuel system issues** - Injector problems, fuel pressure
6. **EVAP system leaks** - Fuel vapor recovery system problems

## Symptoms
- Check Engine light illuminated
- May have no other symptoms initially
- Possible rough idle, poor fuel economy
- Reduced power or acceleration
- Engine may run normally despite the light

## Safe Checks
1. **Gas cap check** - Ensure fuel cap is tight and not damaged
2. **Visual inspection** - Look for obvious leaks or damage
3. **Battery terminals** - Check for corrosion or loose connections
4. **Air filter** - Check if dirty or clogged
5. **Oil level** - Ensure adequate oil level

## Severity Assessment
- **OK to LIMIT** - Usually safe to drive short distances
- Monitor for other symptoms
- Get diagnosis within 1-2 weeks
- **STOP** if accompanied by other warning lights

## Professional Diagnosis Required
- OBD-II diagnostic scan for specific codes
- Professional interpretation of fault codes
- System-specific testing and repair
