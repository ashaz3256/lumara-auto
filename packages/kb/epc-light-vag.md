# EPC Light (VAG Group Vehicles)

## What is EPC Light?
The EPC (Electronic Power Control) light is specific to Volkswagen, Audi, Seat, and Skoda vehicles. It indicates an issue with the electronic throttle control system.

## Common Causes
1. **Throttle body malfunction** - Most common cause, often due to carbon buildup
2. **Accelerator pedal position sensor** - Sensor failure or wiring issues
3. **Throttle position sensor** - Internal sensor failure
4. **Wiring harness issues** - Loose connections or damaged wires
5. **ECU communication problems** - Software or hardware issues

## Symptoms
- EPC light illuminated on dashboard
- Reduced engine power (limp mode)
- Rough idle or stalling
- Poor acceleration response
- Engine may not rev above certain RPM

## Safe Checks
1. **Visual inspection** - Check for obvious wiring damage around throttle body
2. **Battery check** - Ensure battery voltage is above 12.4V
3. **Throttle body cleaning** - Remove and clean throttle body (advanced)
4. **Check connections** - Ensure all electrical connections are secure

## Severity Assessment
- **LIMIT** - Safe to drive short distances with reduced power
- Avoid highway driving or heavy acceleration
- Get professional diagnosis within 24-48 hours

## Professional Diagnosis Required
- OBD-II scan for specific fault codes
- Throttle body calibration
- Sensor replacement if needed
- ECU reprogramming if required
