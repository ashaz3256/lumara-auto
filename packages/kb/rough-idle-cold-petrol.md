# Rough Idle (Cold Start - Petrol)

## What is Rough Idle?
Engine runs roughly or stumbles when idling, especially noticeable during cold starts.

## Common Causes
1. **Dirty air filter** - Restricting airflow
2. **Faulty spark plugs** - Worn or fouled plugs
3. **Dirty throttle body** - Carbon buildup
4. **Mass airflow sensor** - Dirty or faulty sensor
5. **Idle air control valve** - Stuck or dirty
6. **Fuel system issues** - Injectors or fuel pressure

## Symptoms
- Engine shakes or vibrates at idle
- RPM fluctuates up and down
- Stalling when coming to stop
- Poor fuel economy
- Rough acceleration
- Check engine light may illuminate

## Safe Checks
1. **Air filter inspection** - Check if dirty or clogged
2. **Visual inspection** - Look for obvious vacuum leaks
3. **Battery voltage** - Ensure adequate power
4. **Fuel cap** - Ensure tight and not damaged
5. **Throttle body** - Look for excessive carbon buildup

## Severity Assessment
- **OK** - Usually safe to drive
- Monitor for worsening symptoms
- Get diagnosis within 1-2 weeks
- **LIMIT** - If stalling occurs frequently

## Professional Diagnosis Required
- OBD-II diagnostic scan
- Spark plug inspection/replacement
- Throttle body cleaning
- MAF sensor cleaning/testing
- Idle air control valve service
