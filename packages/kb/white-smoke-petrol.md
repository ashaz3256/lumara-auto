# White Smoke from Exhaust (Petrol)

## What is White Smoke?
White smoke from the exhaust can indicate several issues, ranging from normal condensation to serious engine problems.

## Common Causes
1. **Normal condensation** - Cold weather, short trips (harmless)
2. **Coolant leak into combustion chamber** - Head gasket failure
3. **Water in fuel** - Contaminated fuel supply
4. **Cracked cylinder head** - Serious engine damage
5. **Intake manifold gasket leak** - Coolant entering intake system

## Symptoms
- White smoke from exhaust pipe
- Sweet smell (antifreeze) - indicates coolant leak
- Overheating engine
- Loss of coolant without visible leak
- Rough idle or misfires
- White residue on spark plugs

## Safe Checks
1. **Check coolant level** - Look for low coolant in reservoir
2. **Oil inspection** - Check for milky oil on dipstick
3. **Exhaust smell** - Sweet smell indicates coolant leak
4. **Temperature gauge** - Monitor for overheating
5. **Visual inspection** - Look for external coolant leaks

## Severity Assessment
- **STOP** - If sweet smell or overheating present
- **LIMIT** - If coolant level dropping
- **OK** - If only on cold start and dissipates quickly

## Professional Diagnosis Required
- Compression test to check for head gasket failure
- Coolant system pressure test
- Cylinder head inspection
- Engine disassembly if internal damage suspected
