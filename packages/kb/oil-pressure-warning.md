# Oil Pressure Warning Light

## What is Oil Pressure Warning?
The oil pressure warning light indicates dangerously low oil pressure, which can cause severe engine damage within minutes.

## Common Causes
1. **Low oil level** - Most common cause
2. **Oil pump failure** - Not circulating oil properly
3. **Oil filter blockage** - Restricting oil flow
4. **Worn engine bearings** - Excessive clearance reducing pressure
5. **Oil pickup tube blockage** - Debris in oil pan
6. **Oil pressure sensor failure** - False warning (less common)

## Symptoms
- Red oil pressure light illuminated
- Oil pressure gauge reading low
- Engine noise (ticking, knocking)
- Oil light flickering at idle
- Engine may run rough or stall

## Immediate Actions (STOP)
1. **Turn off engine immediately** - Do not continue driving
2. **Check oil level** - Only when engine is cool
3. **Add oil if low** - Use correct grade and amount
4. **Do not restart** - If oil level was adequate
5. **Call for assistance** - Engine may be damaged

## Safe Checks (When Cool)
1. **Oil level** - Check dipstick, add oil if needed
2. **Oil condition** - Look for metal particles or contamination
3. **Oil filter** - Check if loose or damaged
4. **Visual leaks** - Look for oil under the car
5. **Oil pressure sensor** - Check electrical connection

## Severity Assessment
- **STOP** - Never drive with oil pressure warning
- Risk of complete engine failure
- Damage can occur within minutes
- Expensive repairs or engine replacement

## Professional Diagnosis Required
- Oil pressure testing with gauge
- Oil pump inspection/replacement
- Engine bearing inspection
- Oil system cleaning
- Engine rebuild if severe damage
