# Battery/Charging System Issues

## What are Battery/Charging Issues?
Problems with the vehicle's electrical charging system that can leave you stranded.

## Common Causes
1. **Dead battery** - Most common cause
2. **Alternator failure** - Not charging the battery
3. **Battery connections** - Corroded or loose terminals
4. **Belt problems** - Alternator belt broken or loose
5. **Voltage regulator failure** - Over or under charging
6. **Parasitic drain** - Something draining battery when off

## Symptoms
- Battery warning light
- Dim headlights or interior lights
- Slow cranking or no start
- Electrical accessories not working
- Battery dies repeatedly
- Alternator whining noise

## Safe Checks
1. **Battery voltage** - Check with multimeter (should be 12.6V+)
2. **Battery terminals** - Clean corrosion, ensure tight connections
3. **Belt condition** - Check alternator belt for cracks or looseness
4. **Jump start test** - Try with jumper cables
5. **Visual inspection** - Look for obvious damage or leaks

## Severity Assessment
- **LIMIT** - Safe for short trips during daylight
- Avoid long distances or night driving
- Get professional diagnosis within 24-48 hours
- **STOP** - If no response to jump start

## Professional Diagnosis Required
- Battery load testing
- Alternator output testing
- Charging system diagnosis
- Parasitic drain testing
- Belt tension adjustment
